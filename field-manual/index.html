12-Week Progression

Three phases — foundation, growth, intensity

Foundation

Weeks 1–2: 3 sets/exercise, 60 sec rest. Weeks 3–4: 3–4 sets/exercise, 50 sec rest.

Learn all exercisesPerfect form
Build habitDon't miss workouts
Eat consistentlyHit calories daily
Hair routineEstablish wash day

Growth

Weeks 5–6: 4 sets, 45 sec rest, slower tempo. Weeks 7–8: 4–5 sets, 40 sec rest, harder variations.

Add volume4 sets every exercise
Increase reps+3–5 reps / set
Reduce rest45 sec between sets
Hair growthVisible moisture improvement

Intensity

Weeks 9–10: 5 sets, 30 sec rest, weighted where possible. Weeks 11–12: max effort, test all exercises.

Advanced variationsOne-arm push-up prep, pistol squats
Weighted exercisesBackpack with books
Circuit trainingFull body challenge, 5 rounds
Visible resultsNoticeable muscle + definition

Final Rules

1

Consistency beats intensity. Show up every day, even on low-energy days.

2

Eat like it's your job. You won't grow without food.

3

Sleep is training. Muscles grow at night, not in the gym.

4

Hair takes time. 3–6 months to see real growth.

5

Be patient. The mirror lies day to day; photos don't lie month to month.

6

No zero days. Even on rest days, stretch, moisturize, eat well.

7

Track everything. What gets measured gets improved.

This is your complete system. Follow it for 12 weeks and you will see transformation in your body, face, and hair.
